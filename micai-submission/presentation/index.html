The problem with scalar returns

Gt = rt + γ·rt+1 + γ²·rt+2 + ⋯    γ ∈ [0,1)
  • The infinite future is collapsed into one number — all temporal structure is discarded.
  • The discount factor γ is arbitrary: it exists to make the sum converge, not because the task demands it.
  • A reward 10 steps ahead counts for γ10 of its face value — the math shapes the objective, instead of the objective shaping the math.

Consequence: in sacrifice patterns — pay now, gain forever — scalar methods can provably pick the wrong action.

2

Key idea: keep the stream

From any state, record the best achievable reward at every future depth — and never collapse it:

value(s′) = [ v₀, v₁, v₂, … ]   an infinite stream, not a number

Compare states by pointwise stream dominance:

x ≤s y  ⟺  xt ≤ yt  for every  t = 0, 1, 2, …
  • No discount factor. No averaging. No horizon cutoff.
  • The better state must be at least as good at every single future depth.
3

Two coinductive optimality conditions

CoinductiveHomomorphism — strategic

a ≤a b ⟹ value(next(s,a)) ≤s value(next(s,b))

Judge actions by where they lead. Immediate reward is invisible; successor quality is everything.

CoindHomo — tactical

a ≤a b ⟹ action-value(s,a) ≤s action-value(s,b)

The better action must win the immediate payoff and the long-term future — head and tail of the stream.

Decomposition theorem: CoindHomo = CoinductiveHomomorphism + immediate-reward compatibility. Machine-checked.

4

Why “Symmetric”: the full ranking

  • The object of interest is the entire order on the action set — an element of the symmetric group S|𝒜| — not just the argmax.
  • An agent that has understood its environment can say which of its two worst options is less bad. An argmax agent cannot.
  • A top-action-only learner can look competent while its internal model is structurally faulty — until its preferred action disappears.

The full-ranking stance pays operationally:

• learning converges by fixing every violated pair — no exploration mechanism needed

• when the top action becomes unavailable: O(1) fallback, with a verified guarantee

Computing rankings: the Finder

  • The full coinductive condition is undecidable in general — like AIXI, an ideal that organizes practical approximations.
  • The Finder compares finite traces lexicographically at depth n: earlier depths dominate, ties recurse deeper.
  • No discount factor required — the lexicographic order is well-defined at any depth.

Exactness is not assumed — it is verified. Environment Classes prove conditions (e.g. horizon sufficiency) under which finite traces decide the infinite condition.

8

Learning as symmetry restoration

  • A violation: the current ranking disagrees with the ground-truth comparator on one pair, at one state.
  • The repair is a transposition — swap the two actions. Transpositions generate S|𝒜|: learning literally walks the symmetric group.
max-violations ≤ |S| × ( |𝒜| choose 2 )

Swap Monotonicity (verified): each swap fixes its pair, leaves unrelated pairs unchanged, and strictly decreases total violations ⟹ convergence in bounded steps.

Corollary: uniform pairwise sampling suffices — the exploration/exploitation trade-off dissolves. Adaptation to action unavailability: O(1), backed by demote-preserves-dominance.

9

Stochastic extension

  • Environments lift through the finite distribution monad; values become expected-reward streams.
  • Pointwise order is too strict under expectation ⟹ lexicographic coinductive comparison: compare heads, recurse on ties.
  • Beyond expectation: a hierarchy of stochastic dominance relations, all verifiable —
SD[0] = FOSD ⊂ SD[1] = SOSD ⊂ SD[2] = TOSD ⊂ ⋯

Each level captures a broader class of utility functions. Verified rankings compose: product, convolution, sum, scaling — zero extra proof burden for independent subsystems.

Strengths and limits — honestly

Strengths

  • Discount-free optimality criterion
  • Correct in sacrifice patterns where discounting provably fails
  • Everything machine-checked, zero postulates
  • Exploration-free convergent learning; O(1) adaptation

Limits

  • Finite action spaces (continuous: open)
  • Exact traces need the step function — closer to model-based RL / formal planning
  • Validation is by verified instantiation, not benchmark — empirical evaluation is ongoing work
12

Takeaways

  • Optimality = structure preservation, not scalar maximization.
  • The full ranking — an element of S|𝒜| — is the object of knowledge, and it pays: provable convergence, instant adaptation.
  • Verified ≠ small: environment classes carry the proofs to 16.7M-configuration domains.
